The generated artwork establishes recurring archetypes: a caramel Indian pariah dog, a grey tabby cat, and an Indian companion. It is editorial illustration and is not connected to any real listing.

## Generation briefs

### First hello

Create a wide editorial hero illustration for an India-first pet-adoption website. Show a warm Indian adult sitting at ground level with a caramel Indian pariah dog and a grey tabby cat, all exchanging a curious first hello. Use tactile clay-and-layered-paper materials, gentle handmade imperfections, expressive but believable anatomy, warm window light, and a deep near-black background that lets the figures read clearly. Keep the trio grouped toward the right with breathing room for a headline on the left. Use cobalt, marigold, tomato, cream, and leaf green accents. No words, logos, watermarks, collars with text, cages, sadness, glossy 3D plastic, or imitation of a named studio.

### Ordinary home

Create a wide closing editorial illustration using the same tactile clay-and-layered-paper world and recurring characters. In a warm Indian home at golden hour, the caramel pariah dog and grey tabby cat sleep close to their person while the person reads nearby. Make the scene quiet, safe, intimate, and grounded in an ordinary evening rather than a posed adoption advertisement. Leave calm negative space for adjacent copy. Use warm cream, cobalt, tomato, marigold, and leaf green. No words, logos, watermarks, luxury staging, cages, exaggerated sentiment, glossy plastic, or imitation of a named studio.

## Image prompt grammar

Every generated scene defines: product role, emotional beat, recurring characters, tactile paper-clay material, composition, lighting, palette, negative space, export dimensions, and explicit exclusions. Exclude logos, text, watermarks, glossy plastic, imitations of named animation studios, stereotypes, and anatomical errors.

## Future library policy

Prefer original assets. CC0 libraries may supply production inputs only when their license is recorded at the individual asset level and the result is restyled to this system. Approved research sources include Open Peeps, Kenney, Quaternius, and Poly Haven. Do not hotlink assets.

## Delivery rules

- Raster artwork: WebP/AVIF, responsive dimensions, explicit intrinsic size.
- SVG: stable group identifiers for animation, reduced precision, descriptive title where meaningful.
- Audio: local file, optional, lazy loaded, loop-tested, no essential information.
- Preserve source prompts and generation scripts so assets can be reproduced or replaced.
